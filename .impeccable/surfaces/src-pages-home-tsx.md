## Scope and mode

Whole app shell: home `/` (today's picture plus recent days), day detail `/apod/:date`, gallery `/gallery/:month` (old `/archive/*` links redirect), about, not-found. Mode: Experience (the picture leads); about is Read.

## Audience, job, constraints

Astronomy enthusiasts on the daily ritual, portfolio visitors judging craft. Job: TODAY → EXPLORE → SHARE with almost no friction, phone first. Constraints: trilingual UI (es, en, pt-BR) with NASA text untouched and marked `lang="en"`; the day picture is never cropped; no Three.js decoration; nav is only Today and Gallery plus language; the user's brief (2026-09-11) pins navy ground, soft white, slate and one spatial accent, and bans dashboards, sidebars, glass panels, card piles, neon, HUD and constant animation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every screen reads as a page of NASA's 1975 graphics standards manual set on deep navy: a strict 12-column grid, one grotesque in three sizes, flush-left, generous air, one orbital blue, and the day's photograph whole and dominant. It refuses the sci-fi HUD dashboard and the stock dark card gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Navy-black ground #030712, raised #0B1120 only for placeholders and the share menu, soft white #E8ECF2, slate #94A3B8, one orbital blue #416CE6 used as a solid field (the single primary action per screen, the current-nav bar, focus, confirmations). Public Sans, the US federal design system face, in three sizes (display, body, small); rank by weight and colour; tabular figures for dates. Rounded and floating (user steer 2026-09-15): pill controls, a 2.25rem photo frame, 1rem tiles, a pill header, surfaces lifted by soft offset shadows and never glass; no borders between zones, no card piles: zones are separated by air. Raised from the declined hand: three type sizes only (timetable rack); one emphasis per screen (zine); every day reachable by typing its date (teletext); composition recalculates for any aspect ratio, never cropping the day (horizon); the page divides only where the task changes (neon); air, not lines, between zones (daylight section).

STORY: The visitor sees today's picture at once, reads its title and two lines, opens the details or shares it, then explores earlier days in a strict photo grid that never shifts, month by month, in their own language.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The home is one screen with no scrolling (user steer 2026-09-15). Desktop: a floating pill header; the photograph whole in columns 6-12 as the floating card itself (user steer 2026-09-15: the card takes the picture's own shape, as large as fits and centred, rounded, softly shadowed, a glow of its own colours behind; shapes are remembered from any copy that loaded, videos 16:9), its credit on the picture; columns 1-5 hold the NASA title, the date line, a clamped excerpt and Ver detalles (blue) plus Compartir, above a dock of four earlier days; a one-line footer. Phones: the photograph takes whatever height is left above the title, date, a two-line excerpt (dropped on short screens), the two actions side by side, a row of five earlier days and the footer line.

FORM: NASA Graphics Standards Manual (1975) rendered in deep navy, candidate 3 of 7 in re-roll round 1, seed f1d2cffb. Signature interaction: travelling between days slides the photographs along the column axis (View Transitions, direction-aware; swipe on phones, arrow keys on desktop), and a gallery thumbnail grows into the day's photograph. Motion grammar: pictures develop from a blurred preview to sharp; grid-true horizontal slides; nothing moves at rest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
